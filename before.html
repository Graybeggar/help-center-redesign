Frequently Asked Questions - Please Read Everything Carefully Before Contacting Support

Q: How do I reset my password? A: To reset your password you must navigate to the login page and click the link and then check your email and then click the link in the email within 15 minutes or the link expires and you must start over and if you do not receive the email check your spam folder and also make sure you are checking the right email account because the reset email only goes to the email address on file and we cannot send it to a different address for security reasons. Q: Why was I charged twice? A: Duplicate charges may appear as pending authorizations which usually fall off in 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank and if both charges settle you may submit a request with your account email and the last four digits of the card and the dates of both charges and a screenshot if possible and refunds are processed in the order received. Click here

Q: How do I change my email address? A: Account email changes require verification of the current email address and the new email address and if you no longer have access to the current email address you must provide identification and the process takes 2 to 3 business days and during that time you will not be able to sign in. Click here

Q: Why does the two-factor code not arrive? A: Codes are sent by SMS and may be delayed by your carrier and you should wait at least 10 minutes before requesting another code because requesting multiple codes may invalidate earlier codes and if you changed your phone number you must first verify your identity to update the number on file. Q: How do I get a copy of my invoice? A: Invoices are available under account settings and billing history and can be downloaded as PDF files and if you need invoices older than 12 months you must contact support because older invoices are archived. Click here

Q: How do I cancel? A: Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period and you will keep access until that date and no partial refunds are issued for the remaining days unless required by law and you will receive a confirmation email when the cancellation is scheduled. Click here

Q: Why does the app crash? A: Crashes may be caused by an outdated version or insufficient device storage or corrupted cache data and you should update the app and restart your device and clear the cache and if the problem continues include your device model and operating system version when contacting support. Click here

Q: How do I merge two accounts? A: Account merges are performed manually by the support team and require proof that you own both accounts and all data from the secondary account is moved to the primary account and this process cannot be undone. Click here

For all other questions please use the contact form and allow 3 to 5 business days for a response. Do not submit multiple requests about the same issue as this delays processing for everyone.
